About CamPulse

CamPulse is an institutional accountability and feedback platform designed for university campuses, pioneering at Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), Ile-Ife.

Our mission is to eliminate bureaucratic delays, give every student a verified voice, and provide departments with actionable SLA monitoring to fix campus infrastructure, academic bottlenecks, and welfare issues rapidly.

Privacy & Anonymity Protection

When you submit a complaint anonymously, department officers and faculty staff see only "Anonymous Student".

Your true matriculation ID and email are encrypted and isolated strictly for institutional safety and abuse audits, accessible exclusively to the Super Administrator. Sensitive categories like Safety & Security are kept strictly private by default.

Terms of Service

CamPulse is dedicated to civil, constructive university improvement. Users agree not to submit defamatory, fraudulent, or harassing content.

Departments operate under 24-hour urgent and 72-hour standard SLA resolution targets. Rate limits of 5 submissions per hour are enforced to prevent abuse.
